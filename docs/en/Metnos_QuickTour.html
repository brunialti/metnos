01 METNOS AT A GLANCE

An assistant that gets things done.
And shows you how.

Metnos is a personal assistant you install on a computer of your own, at home or in the office. You write to it as you would to a person, from the web page or from Telegram: it prepares a plan, picks the tools, does the work and lets you see every step.

It acts, not just answers

It reads mail and calendars, finds and tidies files, logs into websites, fills in spreadsheets. It works on your PC too.

It stays at home

The language model runs on your server. An external model is used only where you allowed it, and you can see when.

You have the last word

Before moving or deleting many files it shows you the numbers and waits for your yes. And «undo» puts everything back.

Eight requests to begin with

“What appointments do I have tomorrow?”

Calendar · A table with title, start and end.

“Find today’s unread emails that have a PDF attached.”

Mail · Only the messages that match.

“Find the PDFs modified this week in the Documents folder and put them in a spreadsheet with name, date and size.”

Files · The spreadsheet arrives in the chat, ready to download.

“In the photo archive find the pictures of a dog on the beach.”

Photos · Found by what they show, not by file name.

“Search the web for the latest news on the digital product passport and cite the sources.”

Web · A summary of the pages read, with their links.

“Every Monday at 8 send me a summary of the unread emails.”

Reminders · A task that repeats by itself and writes to you.

“Find a pharmacy near here.”

Places · It starts from the last location you sent it from Telegram.

“How much free space is there on my PC?”

PC · The answer comes from the PC, not from the server.

These sentences were tried on the Metnos planner. Functions that use an account need that account’s credentials. All functions, with more examples →

03 THE TOOLS

More than a hundred tools.
Each with its own trade.

Every capability of Metnos is a tool with its own card: what it does, what it needs, what it returns, whether it can be undone. The project calls it an executor; you do not need its name to use it.

Content

Files and folders, spreadsheets and documents, PDFs and scans, archives, installed programs.

Communication and time

Mail, appointments, calendars, contacts, reminders and recurring tasks.

Web, places and images

Web searches, online pages and PDFs, websites with a login, places, photos and people.

Control and collaboration

Computer health, credentials, preferences, approvals; issues and pull requests on GitHub.

Three ways of working

DET · Rules and code

It follows its own code only: same input, same result. Most tools work this way.

LLM · With a model

It uses a language model for part of the work, for example to summarise or describe.

AGENT · Decides along the way

It observes, chooses and acts over several moves, within a mandate and precise limits: browsing websites works like this.

How they look in the chat

In the steps of a collection from the bank website: rule-based tools in grey, tools that decide along the way in green. The $ marks the step that asked an external model, in the cloud, for help: it is not a price. Its absence does not mean the turn used no model: the plan, for instance, is prepared by the local model.

Where they come from: handcrafted written in the project · synthesized built by Metnos and activated with your yes · imported taken from an extension, with recorded provenance · builtin engine functions, such as «undo».

04 HIGHLIGHT · LONG JOBS

The long job keeps going.
You do something else.

Finding duplicates among thousands of photos can take hours. Metnos hands the work to its long-job engine, LRE, and leaves you free.

One sentence

For each folder: find the duplicates, put them in a spreadsheet, move the newer copies. Metnos turns it into a single job, with one piece per folder.

A receipt straight away

The job is registered and goes on even if you close the page. If one folder fails, the others continue; after an interruption it resumes where it was.

One question at the end

Before moving anything it tells you how many files each folder holds and which copy stays, and attaches the spreadsheet. You approve or reject; the question expires after one hour.

The answer finds you

It arrives in the same conversation; if you are not on the page, you also get it on Telegram. Progress and outcome: Settings → System → LRE.

05 HIGHLIGHT · UNDO

One word,
and everything is back.

After your yes, Metnos moved 46 files into three new folders and wrote a spreadsheet. Type «undo»: it reverses the whole request, not just the last step.

What comes back

Files go back where they were. Created folders are removed if they are still empty and still the same. The spreadsheet created by the same request disappears.

Even after a yes

The whole request counts: the part done before the question, the part after your confirmation, the part inside a long job. Metnos reverses it all in reverse order.

Said plainly

The answer says what came back, what did not, and why. If time runs out, another «undo» resumes where it stopped, without counting again.

The boundary

Only what has a recorded reverse operation can be undone. A sent email stays sent: if you want to read it first, ask «show me the text first».

Try it. Write “Create the folder metnos_test”, watch the folder appear, then write “undo”: it disappears, and the answer tells you so.

06 HIGHLIGHT · WEBSITES AND SPREADSHEETS

In goes a website.
Out comes a spreadsheet.

Like a person, with method

Bank transactions, invoices, bills: Metnos opens the website in a browser of its own, logs in, navigates, reads the pages, extracts the rows and fills in the spreadsheet you ask for.

Passwords kept safe

You enter them once, in a protected form. They stay encrypted and reach the browser only at login time: the model never sees them, and they do not belong in the chat.

When a person is needed

If the website asks for an “I am not a robot” check, Metnos tries to pass it; if it cannot, or if a code only you have is needed, it hands you the same browser window and waits until you finish.

A spreadsheet that tells the truth

Complete dates become real dates. The «Notes» sheet says where the data come from and warns when the collection is incomplete. If an external model was needed, the answer says so and the step carries the $ symbol.

Answer too long? It becomes a file. When the result does not fit in the chat, it arrives as an attached spreadsheet or text file with every result, up to 256,000 rows; the chat keeps a short outcome, for example “Results: 18,342 rows. They are all in the attached file”.

07 HIGHLIGHT · NEW TOOLS

What it cannot do,
it learns to do.

When a request ends with “I cannot do this”, Metnos takes note. In quiet moments it builds the missing tool, starting with the most requested ones, puts it to the test and asks you a single question: shall I activate it?

An example

Without a suitable tool, “Which calendars do I have on Google?” gets “I cannot do this”. Metnos notes the gap, builds list_calendars, tests it and asks whether to activate it.

What it may touch

It computes and works on the files you opened to it. On your external accounts, such as Google, it can only read: writing, deleting, sending and sharing stay with the standard tools.

Under observation

If it fails through a defect of its own, it is withdrawn and rewritten. After sixty days without calls it leaves service; «Reactivate» brings it back as it was.

Planned: activation without a question for tools that only read or whose effects can be undone, with a notice and the Withdraw button.

08 HIGHLIGHT · YOUR COMPUTERS

The server and your PC.
One chat.

Pair a PC with your user and Metnos works inside it: it finds and moves files, checks disks and programs. Write “on my PC” and the answer comes from there.

  1. 1 · Pair

    In Settings → System → Devices choose «Another PC», give it a name and set the owner.

  2. 2 · Open the link on the PC

    The Metnos client is installed and creates its own keys. The link is short-lived.

  3. 3 · Ask

    The server sends signed requests; the client checks the tool, runs it in an isolated environment and answers. It finds “Documents” or “Downloads” even if OneDrive has moved them.

At home, not on the Internet. The device channel is meant for the local network or a private network: the port used by the client must not be exposed to the Internet.

09 SAFE, AT HOME

Capable of a lot.
Within clear boundaries.

An assistant that acts needs firm boundaries. In Metnos they are written in the code and checked before every step, not left to the good sense of a model.

The model is yours

Understanding, planning and tools use the model running on your server. An external model is optional, serves precise tasks and shows up as the $ symbol.

A sandbox for every tool

Each tool works in isolation: it writes only where it is allowed; anything else it can see is read-only.

Explicit permissions

Your personal folders are already yours. Any other server folder is granted separately, in Settings → System → Server permissions.

Passwords kept safe

They are typed into a protected form, stay encrypted and never reach the model or the Tutor.

It asks first

Before moving or deleting many items it shows the numbers and waits for your yes. After a long job it always asks, even for a few.

Fixed rules

System commands go through written rules, not through a model: what is forbidden stays forbidden, however politely you ask.

Everyone in their own space

Each person has their own conversations and permissions; the administration pages are for administrators only.

One session at a time

If you open the chat on a second device, Metnos asks which session to keep; the other one becomes read-only.

Open source

The code is public, under the MIT licence: what you read here can be checked line by line.

Everything on record

Every request stays in the turn log, with steps and outcome; Monitor shows times and the models used.

Trust is not something to ask for. It is built with boundaries you can check, and with an «undo» that works.

11 THE TUTOR

Ask Metnos
how to use Metnos.

The Tutor answers “how do I…?” questions from the public guides and the catalogue of your installation. It explains and tells you where to go; to act, make an ordinary request.

What it knows

The interface pages and their paths, the tools actually installed, the guides. If a page does not exist in your installation, it says so instead of inventing a path.

What it does not do

It runs nothing and grants no permission: explaining a page does not give the right to open it. It answers both in the web chat and on Telegram.

Try asking. “Where do I grant access to a server folder?” · “What is on the Changes page?” · “How do I see the progress of a long job?”

How do I mount a NAS folder in Metnos?

A mount makes a NAS share accessible as a local folder without copying its files. On Linux, Metnos can prepare an SMB/CIFS mount when the administrative operation is available and authorised.

“Mount //storage.example.org/Photos in Metnos’s Pictures folder, read-only.”

The address and share are examples. The folder must be accessible to the service account. If credentials are needed, use the protected password field. If the NAS is already accessible, reuse the existing path.

Persistence after a restart requires separate configuration: a successful mount does not guarantee it. Check by listing the files. Connecting a folder does not index it: the first photo search may start preparation in the background. The Tutor explains the procedure but does not mount anything.
