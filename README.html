Metnos

A personal assistant that runs on your hardware — and stops there.

metnos.com  ·  install  ·  AGPL-3.0

Self-hosted, full stop

Runs on your CPU, your GPU, your filesystem. The only network calls it makes are the ones you explicitly authorise.

Writes its own tools

When the catalogue runs out, Metnos synthesises a new executor — declared, signed, sandboxed, reviewable. No restart.

Always undoable

Every state-changing action declares a reverse pattern. One sentence rewinds the last turn.
