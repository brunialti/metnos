# RM-0008 — G8 work plan

Date: 15 September 2026.
Source baseline: `06a9472e`.
Status: **in progress; not F5 certification or activation**.
Assignment and approved principles: [decision record](decision_rm0008_g8_scope_15_9_2026.md).

## 1. Scope

Complete G8 before G9. F6 remains assigned to Codex after F5, but no G10
schema or deletion authority is introduced here. Do not repeat the F4
transition or modify the shared root checkout.

G8 has two outputs: an installation-bound, evidence-derived entry proof and
a lossless, reconciled retirement of name-based lifecycle state. A signed
summary, a green isolated test suite, or copying rows alone is not either
output. The detailed missing contracts remain enumerated in the handover
and decision record; they must be closed before their implementation.

## 2. Immediate prerequisite: faithful terminal replay

This bounded repair strengthens an existing F4 reader; it neither opens F5
nor changes a signer, schema, capability or productive caller.

- Failure location: `executor_birth_operational._decode_terminal_envelope`
  constructs the publication using the request's contract without comparing
  the contract encoded in the signed publication. `_replay_terminal` also
  does not compare the committed claim's `result_binding` with the envelope.
- Observed fact: the serializer includes both contract identity and an exact
  envelope hash in the durable result, but these two bindings are not both
  checked on replay. Existing signature-tampering tests do not cover a
  correctly signed but inconsistent contract or a modified stored hash.
- Causal hypothesis: the reconstructed value can mask an inconsistent signed
  identity; independent postcondition verification then sees the reconstructed
  request identity instead of the contradictory signed fact.
- Expected result: reject either mismatch before postcondition verification,
  checks or publication; preserve the durable record. Valid exact replay,
  historical-key rotation, signed rejection and crash recovery must retain
  their existing behavior.

Contract: schema 2 and the existing signing/hash domains stay unchanged.
The publication's encoded `contract_id` must equal the request's exact
`ContractId.value` before construction. For a committed claim, the stored
`result_binding` must equal `_terminal_binding(encoded)`. A rejected claim
must not be treated as a successful committed publication. No inference from
names, replacement signatures, fallback values or new key is permitted.

Tests: one parametrized negative replay family for the contract, result hash
and terminal-state bindings, followed by the operational/Producer replay and recovery
families whose boundary is affected. Record the red baseline before repair.
This is an isolated productive-code traversal, not a live installation or
an admission qualifying for the F5 threshold. Installed and native Windows
verification remain unproven until actually performed.

## 3. G8 contract candidate for independent review

This section makes the approved principles executable at their owner
boundaries. It is a contract candidate, not an installed authority or a
claim that the complete G8 design has passed independent review. The
remaining freeze items in section 6 must be resolved before their code.

### 3.1 Historical verification and admission predicate — N2/N3

Use separate historical readers, not a historical object passed off as a
current selection. The ownership-chain reader authenticates the F4 anchor
and every relevant successor context; `executor_birth_prepared_root` returns
only the public verifier material pinned by those authenticated contexts.
Never return `SealedAuthoritiesV1`, call the private-key loader and discard
the key afterwards, or require an obsolete signing private key to verify
a historical signature. Historical author rotation must use the historical
public inventory, not equality with today's active author key.

`contract_store` owns complete enumeration of admission receipts and their
generation/source/journal bindings. `executor_birth_producer_store` owns the
matching Producer bytes, issuance and terminal rows. Its historical reader
opens the existing database read-only, requires the supported schema, starts
one read transaction and never calls `_open`, `_migrate` or `_verify_claimed`.
The enclosing certifier must prove a common stable frontier; two independent
SQLite snapshots are not a cross-store snapshot.

The proposed qualifying predicate is the conjunction of:

- a valid historical `AdmissionReceipt` with `kind=admission`;
- revision class in `first_birth`, `code_revision`, `authority_revision`,
  `contract_revision`, and `approved_lifecycle=active`;
- a verified Producer receipt and its capability-specific issuance, with
  exact request, contract, objective, source, issuer and receipt-byte binding;
- a committed, authenticated schema-2 terminal with no error, report outcome
  `admitted`, and publication operation `commit_birth_snapshot`;
- equality of all overlapping signed facts: request, contract, generation,
  predecessor, candidate, semantic core, context, revision class, checks and
  embedded admission bytes, plus the exact terminal hash;
- Producer validity at the signed admission act time, not the database's
  unsigned finalization time or an invented time immediately before expiry;
- authenticated source and journal reread, with no pending ambiguous recovery.

Localizations, equivalent publication, promotion, reactivation,
reattestation, rejected/quarantined results and preexercise do not qualify
under this proposed predicate. They remain in the enumerated evidence with
an exclusion reason. A malformed or unauthenticated item is not silently
discarded to make the remaining inventory appear complete.

Important source distinction: ordinary technical admissions still use the
request-v1 derivation in `executor_birth_bootstrap._request_factory`, even
after an F4 context transition. `ProducerRequestV2` is used by the context
reattestation path. Do not require a forged V2 request for ordinary technical
history. Resolve capability from the context's closed producer table and
authenticated key binding; a mutable `capability_id` string alone is not proof.

Count each admitted act once across receipt ID, request ID and exact
`(ContractId,generation_id)`. Conflicting duplicates fail; exact duplicates,
retries and context reattestations do not add admissions. Count producers
by authenticated `issuer_id`, not operation, key, directory or executor name.

### 3.2 Certification authority and activation — N1/N2

Proposed scope: one dedicated administrative F5 certification identity,
separate from author, Admission, Producer, distribution, cutover, head and
HTTP credentials. It can attest the threshold evidence only; it cannot
publish executors, change lifecycle, grant capabilities or delete objects.
Provisioning belongs to the native administrative installer. No candidate,
model or ordinary runtime request can create, select or replace this identity.

Private custody belongs outside the service-owned roots; Linux requires the
existing administrator-owned storage discipline and mode 0600, Windows an
equivalent native ACL proof. Runtime readers receive public material only.
Reuse existing canonical Ed25519 codecs and protected filesystem primitives;
do not add a general certificate service or a second installation manager.

The bounded dossier must bind policy/schema version, installation identity,
F4 anchor, authenticated chain frontier, observed source/build, complete
owner inventories, admitted/excluded act identities, both cycle IDs/profile,
defect-census/ledger head, public-registry revision and certifier identity.
Lists and counts are derived from verified owner records, never supplied as
authority by the caller. Canonical encoding rejects duplicate/unknown fields,
invalid types, truncation and inconsistent references. Large history is
paged by the owner and completely reconciled before a positive result.

Keep the historical threshold proof separate from current-release activation.
The runtime's fixed-root reader verifies the selected installation/head,
policy compatibility and current revocation state before returning a sealed
activation object. A later release does not inherit activation merely because
it descends from the certified one. Neither object hashes a manifest that
already includes that object's own digest. No permissive v1 compatibility
wrapper is needed: `load_f5_activation` has no productive caller today.

### 3.3 Complete cycles and defect evidence — N4/N5

Use the existing external HTTP certification harness where it covers the
required boundary. Register the exact versioned case/profile digest and
expected postconditions before starting; do not call two selected successful
turns a complete cycle. Fixture paths, actors and effects must remain isolated
and non-personal; no external messages or new paid API is authorized.

Record every cycle start and terminal outcome, including failure and
interruption, in sequence. The qualifying pair consists of two adjacent
complete successes on the same authenticated source/catalog/profile. A
profile or base change starts a different sequence; an unexplained difference
blocks qualification. Record real turn references and independently observed
postconditions, not just a harness return code.

Initialize the defect ledger from the known RM-0008 findings, including the
terminal-replay repair in section 2. Use an append-only, authenticated record
of opening, closing and reopening. A closure needs causal evidence, affected
tests, installed verification where required and independent review. A
developer's empty list or a scalar zero cannot establish completeness.
Reopening a relevant defect invalidates a positive current-frontier result.

### 3.4 Lossless migration and retirement — N6/N7/N8

The new epoch store belongs to the service and resolves its path through
central configuration. Version 2 cannot silently gain new meanings: freeze
version 3 explicitly before implementing historical associations and the
cutover record. Keep original copied rows immutable; store any later exact
association and its evidence separately. Retrying preservation must verify
the original bytes even after a legitimate resolution was appended.

Copy all `executor_stats`, `executor_history` and `proposal_promote` rows,
including duplicates/history, with source identity, schema fingerprint,
stable row order, count and digest, followed by independent destination
reread. Preserve source databases. `unresolved` is valid for history without
proof; it is not permission to drop effective restrictions or pending work.
New current epochs come from authenticated current generations, with zero
inherited counters and no inferred historical `name -> current` mapping.

Prefer a short, explicitly managed maintenance window for the one-time
migration over a continuously running synchronization subsystem. The writer
census must cover direct SQL as well as aging/promoter APIs, all services,
scheduled callbacks and administrative commands. The barrier must remain
effective across interruption and cover already-open database connections.
Individual SQLite locks or an in-memory flag do not establish that barrier.

Before disabling a legacy reader, determine which stored restrictions are
still effective against the authenticated current catalog. Preserve each
effective restriction with an explicit exact-generation disposition; if the
binding cannot be established, stop that cutover. A new present-time
disposition must not be misrepresented as proof of a historical association.

Drain or explicitly dispose of open promotion/grace/review work before
retirement. Never turn an already active generation into preexercise.
No live row is automatically discarded because its old mechanism is retiring.
Only after complete reconciliation, necessary dispositions and durable
cutover may legacy writers become unreachable/refuse mutations and legacy
views become non-decisional. Crash/retry must not reopen those writers.

## 4. Implementation sequence

1. Close N1–N5 into exact public historical reader, evidence, certification
   and activation contracts. Do not expose `SealedAuthoritiesV1` or call
   `_open`/`_verify_claimed` to perform supposedly non-mutating historical
   verification. Derive validity time from authenticated act evidence.
2. Close N6–N8 using the actual source/owner/writer census and approved
   lossless/disposition rules. Freeze the next epoch schema and recovery
   protocol before implementing it; retain unresolved history without
   fabricating associations or importing old counters into new epochs.
3. Implement the closed contracts in causal slices, with risk-to-test
   mapping, boundary review and independent review at each new authority.
4. Obtain real installed evidence and complete G8 exit checks. Missing
   producers, cycles, defect closures or dispositions remain blockers,
   never synthetic evidence. Only then enter G9.
