## Findings and disposition

### P2 — an admitted recovery hint could replay as a rejected terminal

`runtime/executor_birth_operational.py::_replay_terminal` compared publication
presence but not the signed outcome/error with the stored state. An authentic
hint placed in a rejected row returned admitted, no error and no publication.
The reviewer reproduced this with real functions and an ephemeral key in
memory. It was a pre-existing residual omission, not introduced by `04aa1b14`.

Fixed locally by checking the committed or rejected outcome family and the
operational error binding. The reviewer inspected the revised diff and found
no additional blocker in this bounded repair. Genuine preexercise, rejection,
recovery and reuse of a receipt already persisted remain supported. Strict
validation of the complete future certification dossier is a separate task.

### P1 — historical original bytes were incorrectly assumed available

`contract_store.GENERATION_FILES` preserves manifest, signature and linguistic
state. `executor_birth_authoring.cleanup_transaction` removes the temporary
journal and backup. Release evidence stores distribution metadata/signature;
release sources may recover some code, but do not guarantee each act's journal
or transformed candidate. Coverage of the 69 metadata candidates is unproven.

Disposition: Roberto explicitly approved the first option after a plain-language
explanation: verify signed receipts, exact bindings and persistent state,
and disclose unavailable originals. The detailed decision is in
`../design/decision_rm0008_g8_scope_15_9_2026.md`. No threshold is certified by
this decision; per-act verification and inventory remain required.

### P1 — public historical verification must be transitively public-only

`executor_birth_prepared_set.load_authority_set_v1` loads private material and
compares the historical author with today's active author. Its
`authority_registry_v1` also reaches private loaders. Merely returning a public
projection does not make these paths suitable historical readers.

Acceptance: use the exact authenticated historical context/inventory without
private-key reads; succeed with private files inaccessible and the active
author rotated. Reuse the persistent public material in the frozen context
and owner-local authentication of immutable generation payloads. Do not pass
a historical generation to a current-selection reader.

Disposition: implemented the closed reader contract from work-plan §3.5.
The Ownership public loader now uses marker/set/material/public files without
loading private material. Historical target contexts are selected only from
the fixed authenticated chain, reread around the acquisition. Returned views
are immutable evidence, not operational capabilities. Local tests deny
private reads and rotate the author; installed and native Windows evidence
remain separate open checks.

### P3 — historical registry version was unnecessarily fixed

The first reader diff required registry component version `"1"`, contrary to
the closed contract's independence from the current component catalog. The
reviewer identified this as a compatibility limitation, not a trust bypass;
no installed record exercising it was claimed.

Fixed after a discriminating red test using the real builder with a different
recorded version. The decoder still verifies the material schema, context
identity and registry digest with that recorded version. The reviewer checked
the final change and the public/immutable boundary, finding no additional
blocker. This static review did not repeat any tests.

### P1 — persisted Producer protocols must not be conflated

`executor_birth_reattestation._execute` finalizes with a receipt-result binding
and no schema-2 terminal. Such acts must be authenticated under their own
protocol and excluded from technical-admission counts. Ordinary technical
admissions still derive request-v1 identities. Their terminal may omit the
embedded admission when the commit publisher reuses a durable receipt.

Acceptance: derive the actual request protocol, verify exact receipt/issuer/
capability bindings, independently reread the durable admission when needed,
and never manufacture V2 requests or reject valid history merely for having
a different terminal representation.
