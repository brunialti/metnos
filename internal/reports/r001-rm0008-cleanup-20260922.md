## Retirement decision

Keep the 16 observed legacy entry points retired. Their `.retired-v1` contents
are byte-identical to the corresponding original Git blobs; every comparison
passed before recording them. No source bytes are lost, no entry point is
reactivated, and the original blobs remain available in the parent commit.

The explicit retirement action is documented in
`runtime/executor_birth_legacy_neutralizer.py::_revoke_v1` on the F5 branch
(`2ac90907`), with the plan owned by `executor_birth_legacy_retirement.py`.
The historical F4 handovers record retirement of the principal installation's
legacy paths during the successful transition. The filename pattern alone
does not establish an accidental test against production. Restoring executable
entry points merely to clean Git would therefore be an unverified reversal
of that transition; this reconciliation deliberately preserves observed state.
