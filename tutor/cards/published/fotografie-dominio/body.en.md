{catalog_summary}

To search, name the archive or folder and describe what you want to find. Metnos reuses the existing persistent index. If it is missing, the first content search automatically requests indexing through LRE: no manual start is needed. LRE must be enabled, the archive accessible and the models configured. If a check fails, preparation does not start and Metnos explains why.

Work continues in the background and large archives may take hours. The initial receipt does not mean the index is ready: wait for the outcome in the LRE console or through available associated channels, then repeat the search. After completion, subsequent searches reuse the index and are normally faster.

Example: `In the 2025 Photos archive, find pictures of Roberto by the sea.`

Web search and Google Photos are separate capabilities: name them explicitly if you want to use them instead of the local archive.
