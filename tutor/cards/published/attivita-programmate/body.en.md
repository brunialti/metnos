You do not need to open the console or learn a special syntax. Ask Metnos in natural language, stating **what to do** and **when**.

Example: `Every morning at 8, read my email and flag deadlines and urgent messages.`

Metnos will show the resulting schedule and apply the normal gates. Name a delivery channel if you want the result sent elsewhere. For a one-off run, omit the recurrence and ask it to read the messages directly.
